# Handling-editor desk-reject simulation

## Scope

The simulation used only the title, abstract, Introduction, Figure 1, and cover letter. It is a pre-submission risk audit, not a prediction of editorial outcome.

| Risk | Status | Evidence-based response | Remaining exposure |
|---|---|---|---|
| Secondary public-data reanalysis | PARTIALLY_RESOLVED | Prespecified 3,974-gene comparison, robustness battery, frozen negative results, and source-data package are explicit. | Novelty still depends on clear positioning. |
| Proteomics contribution appears limited | PARTIALLY_RESOLVED | Protein24 is the endpoint; full processed matrix and independent DIA study are described. | No new raw-DIA reprocessing. |
| Effect size is small | RESOLVED_BY_LANGUAGE | Abstract/title use modest and incremental language with exact effect sizes. | Small effects remain a reviewer issue, appropriately. |
| Different studies are treated as a time series | RESOLVED | Cross-study temporal triangulation and non-longitudinal limitation are stated repeatedly. | No sample-level longitudinal claim. |
| Novel biological insight is unclear | PARTIALLY_RESOLVED | The new contribution is the layer-level incremental-information test, not a pathway claim. | Program-level evidence is non-confirmatory. |

The remaining desk risk is positioning, not an unaddressed scientific error.
